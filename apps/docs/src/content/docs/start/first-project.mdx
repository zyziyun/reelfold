In this walkthrough you turn one talking-head clip into a tight, captioned vertical short for YouTube Shorts and TikTok, with a cover and post copy for each. It takes about five minutes of your time; the machine does the rest.

## Before you start

- Reelfold is installed: [the Mac app](/docs/start/install-mac/) or [the Claude Code skill](/docs/start/install-skill/).
- You have one talking-head recording, one to three minutes long. A vertical phone clip works best; a horizontal webcam recording is reframed to 9:16 around your face.
- Optional: a few terms you say that speech recognition may get wrong (product names, jargon). Mention them in your request.

## Make the short

<Tabs syncKey="form">
<TabItem label="In the Mac app">

<Steps>

1. **Describe it.** On Home, type your request into the box and drop in the clip:

   > Tighten this talking-head clip: cut the pauses, filler words and repeats, speed it up 1.1×, add captions and a progress bar, no hook. Export for YouTube Shorts and TikTok with a cover and post copy.

   Check the platform chip under the box (it applies to this request only), then click **Make it**.

2. **Let it run.** The request becomes a project and All projects opens on it. The [live run page](/docs/app/live-run/) shows what it is doing, the time left and the pieces as they appear. You can leave; Home's **Running** list keeps track.

3. **Fillers are judged for you.** Clear fillers (um, uh, stutters) are always cut. On [Autopilot](/docs/app/autopilot/) the AI also judges the unsure ones ("like", "you know", a restarted sentence, a retake) and records what it cut and kept. After the cut, Reelfold re-transcribes the result to check that no real word was lost.

4. **See what the AI decided.** On the project page, **What the AI decided** lists each choice with its reason: the filler cuts, the opening, the cover. Press **Change** on any of them; the question goes to your Inbox, and only what depends on it is made again.

5. **Open the finished clip.** It is **Ready to post**. Click it to change the cover, captions or versions, or ask the chat ("bigger captions", "a calmer cover"). See [Edit a finished clip](/docs/app/clip-page/).

6. **Publish.** You get one file per platform, each with its own cover, title, description and tags. **Schedule** puts it on the calendar; at its time Reelfold opens the platform's upload page in its built-in browser and fills in the file and the copy. You press publish.

</Steps>

Prefer to approve each step? Switch to **Ask me first** (Settings › General › New projects). The button then reads **Make a plan**: you check the plan, start one clip to preview, and confirm the unsure filler cuts yourself in the Inbox under **Confirm the filler cuts**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="With the Claude Code skill">

<Steps>

1. **Open Claude Code in the clip's folder** and say what you want:

   > Tighten talk.mp4: cut the pauses, filler words and repeats, speed it up 1.1×, add captions and a progress bar, no hook. Export for YouTube Shorts and TikTok with a cover and post copy.

   The skill routes this to the [talkinghead workflow](/docs/reference/workflows/talkinghead/). Claude may ask which editing style you want (a calm notes style, a punchy fast-cut style, or a mix).

2. **Answer the cleanup review sheet.** Claude transcribes the clip and shows a numbered sheet: rows to confirm first, then what will be cut automatically, the pauses to squeeze and what is kept. Each row shows the words around the cut. Reply with the numbers:

   ```text
   approve 3,5,9 keep 7
   ```

   `approve` cuts those rows too; `keep` leaves them in (it works on automatic rows as well). `approve all` accepts every row waiting for confirmation, and ranges like `approve 2-4` work. After the cut, the result is re-transcribed; if a real word went missing, Claude tells you where and keeps that edit instead.

3. **Check the preview.** Claude renders a few preview stills, then the full video. Ask for changes in plain words ("bigger captions", "drop the progress bar").

4. **Cover and post copy.** Claude picks candidate frames for the cover, renders it, and writes a title, description and tags within each platform's limits.

5. **Export per platform.** One clean master becomes one file per platform, with captions placed clear of each app's buttons and loudness set to its target. Under the hood this is `python -m vstudio.export` with `--platforms youtube-shorts,tiktok`.

</Steps>

Publishing from the skill is up to you: upload the files with the post copy it wrote.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

<Aside type="tip">
The cleanup step is the same tool in every workflow that keeps original speech, so the reply format above also works for podcasts, lectures and vlogs. Details: [Speech cleanup](/docs/reference/engine/cleanup/).
</Aside>

## What you end up with

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| One video per platform | 9:16, captions inside the platform's safe area, loudness at the platform's target |
| One cover per platform | Sized for that platform's feed |
| Post copy per platform | Title, description and tags checked against the platform's limits |

## Change something afterwards

Every finished clip can be edited again in plain words: "trim the first two seconds", "make the captions yellow", "add an Instagram version". Each change is one undo step. See [Output edits](/docs/concepts/output-edits/).

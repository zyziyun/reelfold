---
title: Add or replace a voice-over
description: "Add narration to a finished video, choose an installed voice, replace a previous full narration and review the new audio and captions."
---

Open a finished clip and ask its chat for narration. Say the language, whether it covers the opening or the whole video, and the tone:

> Add a calm English voice-over to the opening. Keep the original sound under it and lower the music.

> Replace the full Chinese narration with this script. Keep the timing and use a voice already installed on this Mac.

## Choose a voice

Reelfold chooses from the voices available to the current project and language. Local narration uses downloaded models; hosted voices need the corresponding provider configured. See [Voices](/docs/guides/voices/) to download or change a voice.

## Replace a take

A new full narration that overlaps an earlier full narration replaces that earlier narration and its captions. It does not stack both voices. **Undo** brings back the previous take. An opening-only narration applies to its requested range.

## Review the render

Open the clip and let any pending render finish. Play the resulting video in **Transcript** or **Timeline** to hear the updated mix. Check the words, pronunciation, narration level, original sound and captions together. Holding **C** in the editor previews the original source, so it will not include a newly added narration.

If rendering stops, keep the last good output and use **Try again** after resolving the reported cause. Narration is an edit to the output; it does not overwrite your original footage.

---
title: Make several videos from a folder
description: "Turn a folder or SD card into a themed set of videos, with per-platform lengths, language versions, individual retries and collection edits."
---

Start on **Home**, add your folder or SD card, and turn on **Several videos**. Describe the set, including its count, languages and destinations:

> Make 10–12 travel videos from this folder, each with a different story. English for YouTube and Chinese for Xiaohongshu. Keep the original sound where it matters.

## Review the set

Reelfold surveys the footage and groups it into stories. The plan gives each video a theme, kind and length for its platforms. On **Ask me first**, review the plan before starting; on **Autopilot**, it continues by itself.

Rename a proposed video, change its kind or duration, drop it, or select two proposals to merge. The amount of usable footage limits how many distinct videos can be made. If the requested minimum cannot be reached, the plan says so.

## Follow each version

Open the collection in **All projects**. Each video stays inside the collection, with its language and platform versions beneath it.

| State | Next step |
|---|---|
| Planning or running | Follow the current step and progress. |
| Made | **Open** the finished version to review or edit. |
| Partly made | Open a finished version, or retry the specific failed version. |
| Failed | Read the reason beside that version and choose **Try again**. |

Retrying a failed version preserves the completed versions. A disk-space failure needs free space before another attempt; a model or provider failure needs that service available. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/help/troubleshooting/).

## Edit the collection in plain words

Use the collection chat to ask for a change across the set or on one video:

- “Make the music quieter across all these videos.”
- “Re-cut the river video with a slower opening.”
- “Make two more videos from the footage we have not used.”
- “Bring this set up to 12 videos, with a different angle for each.”

Check the resulting plan and completed outputs. New requests still depend on what the source can support.

## Lengths and languages

A long YouTube version and a short TikTok version can have different lengths. **Settings › Publishing › Video length** sets your preferred minimum and maximum per platform. Name the language for each destination in your request; English and Chinese versions are reviewed independently.

---
title: Record your camera, microphone and screen
description: "Record a lesson or talking-head take in Reelfold with a teleprompter, screen sharing, retakes and transcript editing."
---

On **Home**, choose **Record yourself** under the starting points. You can speak freely or prepare a script for the teleprompter.

## Before recording

1. Choose the camera and microphone and check the microphone level.
2. For a software demo or lesson, choose a screen or window from **Screen**.
3. Adjust the prompter speed and text size. Preview mirroring and mirrored text are separate options.
4. Allow camera, microphone and screen-recording access when macOS asks. If access is denied, open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, change the relevant permission, and follow the system's restart instructions.

## Make a take

Press the record button or **Space**. **P** pauses or resumes. With a script, use **↑ / ↓** to move between lines and **⌘R** to mark that you are saying a line again. Stop to save the take, then open it in the editor. You can keep several takes and choose which one to use.

If you need to change the shared window, save the current take first and start another. Before a long lesson, make a short test and check that picture and sound are present.

## Edit and finish

The recording opens in the clip editor, where local transcription supports word-based edits. Remove unwanted words, correct caption text, or select a line to **Re-record** or **Add after**. Review the join and the sound before finishing.

Describe the result you want, pick the platforms, and choose **Finish — make my video**. Plain-file export is also available. See [Edit a finished clip](/docs/app/clip-page/) for transcript editing, pickups and export to other editors.

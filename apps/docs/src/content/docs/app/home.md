Home is where Reelfold opens. It answers three questions at a glance: what needs you, what is running, and what goes out today. Above them is the box where you start a new video in one sentence.


The sidebar has **Home**, **All projects**, **Publish** and **Settings**. Waiting questions and reviews live in All projects; its badge shows how many need you. Use ⌘K to jump to a project, clip, post or setting.

## Start a video

Type what you want in plain words and add files or a folder (drop them anywhere on the card):

> Cut this 1-hour talk into 5 YouTube Shorts and 5 Xiaohongshu clips, about a minute each.

- **Where it goes.** The platform chip under the box applies to this request only. Pick other platforms for this one video, or press **Make this my default** to keep them for new projects. International platforms are listed first.
- **Make it** sends the request. With [Autopilot](/docs/app/autopilot/) (the default), the request becomes a project at once and runs to finished clips; it shows up in All projects, which opens on it. With **Ask me first**, the button reads **Make a plan** and the plan waits for your Start.
- **No files?** That is fine for explainers, series, scripts, AI shorts and slides. Links are read first: web pages, public Notion pages and exported Notion folders. See [Course videos from notes](/docs/guides/course-from-notes/).

## Make a set from a folder

Turn on **Several videos**, name the count, platforms and languages, and optionally choose the AI and model for this request. The collection is planned together, with individual progress and retries for each language or platform version. See [Make several videos](/docs/guides/several-videos/).

## Suggestions from your work

Under the box, a row of chips suggests what to do next. They come from real signals on this Mac, not from a template:

| Chip | When it shows |
|---|---|
| **New recording** | A new video landed in a folder Reelfold watches (Settings › General › Folders to look in) |
| A request you repeat | You asked for the same kind of video more than once; one click fills the box again |
| **Share "…" on …** | A finished video is out on some of your platforms but not all of them |
| **Next …** | A series is due for its next episode |
| **Finish …** | A project still has clips left to make |

**More from your work** shows the rest. A first-time Home shows starting points instead (clips from a long video, a talking-head edit, a course, an explainer).

## Inbox

The top of the [Inbox](/docs/concepts/batch-review/) shows the first few things waiting on you, with an estimate of your time ("3 need you · about 4 min"). Each row has one button for the next step: **Pick cover**, **Review**, **Decide**, **Fix** (for a run that stopped) or **Add files**. What you do here can be undone from the notice that follows. The attention link opens all waiting items in **All projects**.

## Running

Each running job shows its current step in words (transcribe, cut, captions, render, check, copy), a progress bar and the time left. Up to two projects run at once; others wait their turn and survive a restart. **Watch all** opens every running job side by side with its live log; see [The live run page](/docs/app/live-run/).

## Going out today

Today's posts, in time order, each with its platform and state: **Scheduled**, **Ready to post**, **Waiting on** a platform, or **Posted** (with views where the platform reports them). Click one to open it in [Publish](/docs/guides/scheduling-publishing/).

When nothing runs and nothing needs you, Home says **All clear** and names the next post.

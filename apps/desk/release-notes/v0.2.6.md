## Reelfold 0.2.6 · 千剪 0.2.6

Make the Studio fit the way you work.

### Highlights

- **Resize everything**: drag any divider — the video list, the player vs the transcript, the AI panel, the editor's
  splits. Double-click resets; your sizes are remembered.
- **Fold the list** to a slim strip with ⌘B when you want room for one video; fold its groups too.
- **Roomier header**: a bigger title with the project and length above it; the full title on hover.
- **Back where you were**: switching videos keeps each column's scroll position.
- Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple (macOS, Apple silicon); Windows x64 is an unsigned preview.

### 更新（中文）

- 所有分栏都能拖动调整：视频列表、播放器和逐字稿、AI 面板、编辑器的上下分栏；双击恢复默认，大小会记住。
- 想专心看一条时，⌘B 把列表收成一条窄边；列表分组也能折叠。
- 标题区更宽松：标题更大，上方是项目和时长；悬停显示完整标题。
- 切换视频时，每一栏都回到你上次看的位置。
- macOS（Apple 芯片）已签名并经 Apple 公证；Windows x64 为未签名预览版。

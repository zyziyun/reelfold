# Reelfold 0.2.9

- Learn and create in one workspace: keep materials, notes, camera commentary and screen sharing together, then make an editable video from your own thoughts.
- Improve multilingual caption and cover layout with native text shaping and language-aware wrapping.
- Save recordings reliably after camera, microphone or shared-window interruptions, including their final data.
- Reopen the main window from the Window menu and choose whether to share content with cloud AI.
- Use the mirrored public-domain music library, per-platform video lengths, improved narration and editing recovery.

macOS: Apple silicon, signed and notarized. Windows: x64 unsigned preview; Windows may show a SmartScreen warning.

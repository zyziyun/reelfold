## Reelfold 0.2.7 · 千剪 0.2.7

Turn your course notes into episodes.

### Highlights

- **Notes → course episode**: drop lecture notes or an exported Notion folder and ask for a course video; Reelfold
  drafts an episode of the length you asked for, narrates it in Chinese (or English) and animates it.
- **AI presenter (optional)**: connect Hedra in Settings › Video generation; nothing is spent without your OK.
- **Watch it work**: a calm progress page with a live pane that shows what Claude Code is doing, step by step;
  "Watch all" tiles every running job.
- **Tidy the list**: archive, delete, rename or retry any row (⋯ or right-click); one row per video.
- **Vertical that follows the subject**: landscape footage made for TikTok, Reels or Xiaohongshu is cropped around
  people and what moves, with a smooth camera; blurred fill only where a shot can't be cropped.
- **Real post copy**: each platform gets a title, body, hashtags, place and music credit.
- **Free offline voice** (Apple silicon): no OpenAI key needed for narration; a one-time 330 MB download.
- **See how it's made**: pieces appear as they are made; the project chat is back on every page; click a ready clip
  to edit it.
- **Per-request platforms**: picking platforms for one request no longer changes your default.
- Fixes: finished projects read Ready to post with their cover; colour-graded videos (e.g. travel vlogs) render again; course requests no longer stop at the script step, failures say why, Try again really re-runs, and new
  requests open right after you send them.
- Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple (macOS, Apple silicon); Windows x64 is an unsigned preview.

### 更新（中文）

- 讲稿 → 课程视频：拖入讲稿或导出的 Notion 文件夹，说要做课程视频，千剪按你要的时长起草一集，中文（或英文）配音并做成动画。
- AI 出镜（可选）：在「设置 › 视频生成」连接 Hedra；没有你的确认不会花钱。
- 看得见的进度：简洁的进度页，加一个实时窗口显示 Claude Code 每一步在做什么；「全部查看」平铺所有正在运行的任务。
- 整理列表：任意一行都能归档、删除、改名或重试（⋯ 或右键）；一个视频只显示一行。
- 竖屏跟着主体走：横屏素材做 TikTok、Reels、小红书竖屏时，围绕人物和运动的东西裁切，镜头平滑；只有裁不了的镜头才用模糊背景。
- 真正的发布文案：每个平台都有标题、正文、话题、地点和音乐署名。
- 免费离线配音（Apple 芯片）：配音不再需要 OpenAI key；首次使用下载 330 MB。
- 看得见制作过程：做出来的片段随时出现；每个项目页都有对话；点已完成的片段直接进入编辑。
- 平台按单次请求选：给这次请求选平台不再改动你的默认设置。
- 修复：做完的项目显示「可以发布」并带封面；带调色的视频（如旅行 vlog）恢复正常渲染；课程请求不会再卡在脚本这一步；失败会说明原因；「重试」真的会重跑；发送后直接打开新请求。
- macOS（Apple 芯片）已签名并经 Apple 公证；Windows x64 为未签名预览版。

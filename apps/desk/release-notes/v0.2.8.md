## Reelfold 0.2.8 · 千剪 0.2.8

The open-source workbench for creators: create, edit and publish — many videos at once.

### Highlights

- **Several videos from one folder**: point it at a folder or an SD card and say "make several videos"; it surveys the
  footage, proposes a plan, and makes them as one group you can follow row by row.
- **Import a finished video** (from Reelfold, CapCut/剪映, Final Cut…) and keep editing it, with its footage.
- **A more premium look, no flicker**: cinema-style titles and labels, clean transitions, matched exposure; YouTube
  always in English, Chinese platforms in Chinese.
- **Better covers**: the best frame, the subject cut out, the title placed clear of it; three YouTube variants.
- **A natural free Chinese voice** (Qwen3-TTS, offline, optional download in Settings › Voice).
- **Choose your AI**: pick the model (`/model`), and use Gemini CLI, Qwen Code, Kimi, CodeBuddy, OpenCode, Cursor or
  China-friendly keys (DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, GLM, Doubao).
- **Export for editing software** (Premiere, Resolve, 剪映/CapCut, OTIO); your hand edits survive AI re-runs.
- **The editor brain**: how each project is cut and why, per shot; suggested AI shots with the price, never without
  your OK. Beat-accurate cuts, smooth jump cuts and real slow motion.
- Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple (macOS, Apple silicon); Windows x64 is an unsigned preview.

### 更新（中文）

- 一次做多条视频：指向文件夹或 SD 卡，说「做几条视频」，它先看素材、出方案，再作为一个分组逐条做完。
- 导入已做好的视频（千剪、剪映、Final Cut 等）接着改，可关联原素材。
- 画面更高级、不再屏闪：电影感的标题与地名，干净的转场，统一曝光；YouTube 一律英文，中文平台用中文。
- 更好的封面：挑最好的一帧、抠出主体、标题避开主体；YouTube 三选一。
- 更自然的免费中文配音（Qwen3-TTS，离线，可在「设置 › 配音」下载）。
- 自选 AI：可选模型（`/model`），支持 Gemini CLI、Qwen Code、Kimi、CodeBuddy、OpenCode、Cursor，以及 DeepSeek、通义、Kimi、智谱、豆包等国内 key。
- 导出到剪辑软件（Premiere、Resolve、剪映/CapCut、OTIO）；你手动改过的地方 AI 重做时会保留。
- 「怎么剪」：每个项目、每个镜头为什么这样剪；可生成的镜头会标价，没有你的确认不会花钱。卡点更准、跳剪平滑、真正的慢动作。
- macOS（Apple 芯片）已签名并经 Apple 公证；Windows x64 为未签名预览版。
